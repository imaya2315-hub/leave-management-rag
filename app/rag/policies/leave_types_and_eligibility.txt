Leave Types and Eligibility

The company offers three types of leave to all confirmed, full-time employees: Annual Leave, Sick Leave, and Casual Leave.

Annual Leave: Every employee is entitled to 20 Annual Leave days per calendar year. Annual Leave should be planned in advance and requested at least 5 working days before the intended start date wherever possible, so managers can plan for coverage. Annual Leave can be taken in blocks of one or more working days.

Sick Leave: Every employee is entitled to 10 Sick Leave days per calendar year. Sick Leave can be applied for on the same day it is needed and does not require 5 days' advance notice. For Sick Leave of 3 or more consecutive working days, a medical certificate should be shared with HR within 3 working days of returning to work.

Casual Leave: Every employee is entitled to 5 Casual Leave days per calendar year, intended for short personal matters that come up with little notice. Casual Leave cannot be taken for more than 2 consecutive working days at a time without manager approval in advance.

Eligibility: New employees become eligible to apply for Annual and Casual Leave after completing 90 days of employment (their probation period). Sick Leave is available from an employee's first working day, since it cannot reasonably be planned for.

All leave requests are validated against the employee's current leave balance at the time of application, and again at the time of manager approval, since the balance may have changed between the two.
