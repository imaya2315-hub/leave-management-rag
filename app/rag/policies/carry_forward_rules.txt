Leave Carry-Forward Rules

Unused Annual Leave: Up to 5 unused Annual Leave days may be carried forward into the next calendar year. Any Annual Leave balance beyond 5 days that remains unused at the end of the calendar year is forfeited and does not carry forward. Carried-forward Annual Leave must be used within the first 3 months (by March 31) of the following year, after which it is forfeited.

Unused Sick Leave: Sick Leave does not carry forward. Any unused Sick Leave balance is reset to the standard 10 days at the start of each calendar year.

Unused Casual Leave: Casual Leave does not carry forward. Any unused Casual Leave balance is reset to the standard 5 days at the start of each calendar year.

Employees who join partway through the calendar year receive a pro-rated Annual Leave and Casual Leave balance based on their remaining months in that year, rounded to the nearest whole day. Sick Leave is granted in full regardless of joining date.

Employees leaving the company are paid out for any unused, carried-forward Annual Leave balance as part of their final settlement. Unused Sick Leave and Casual Leave are not paid out on exit, since they do not carry forward.
